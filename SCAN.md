# Room scan: how to film it

Film an empty room with the phone. The server checks coverage afterward. There is no live signal.

## Settings
- 1080p, 30 frames per second, no zoom.
- Lock exposure and focus: tap and hold a wall until the lock appears.
- Room empty, no people in frame.

## Four steps (done in order)
1. Walk the walls once. Stay about one metre from them, phone at chest height, aimed at the wall.
2. Walk back through the middle, zigzagging, pointing at the centre and the walls.
3. At each corner, tilt the phone up at the ceiling for three seconds.
4. Crouch to about knee height. Go around once, pointing at the floor and lower walls.

## Stop rule
Stop when all four steps are done and you are back where you started, facing the first wall. Expect two to four minutes.

## After upload
The server checks coverage. If a wall or the ceiling is missing, film a 30-second clip of that part and add it.
